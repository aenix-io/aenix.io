What is AenixCon

A vendor conference that respects the community.

AenixCon is where the people building open clouds compare notes. One track goes deep on distributed systems, storage, networking, virtualization and AI infrastructure. The other is honest business: migration stories, cloud economics and production cases with numbers. Every session should leave you with something you can use.The people building open clouds compare notes: deep engineering in one track, honest business cases in the other. No slideware marathons — every session should leave you with something you can use.
