What is AenixCon

A vendor conference that respects the community

AenixCon is where the people building open clouds compare notes. One track goes deep on the engineering — distributed clusters, storage, networking, KubeVirt, GPUs and the guts of Cozystack and the CNCF projects around it. The other track is honest business: how real teams left VMware, launched public clouds, monetized hosting, and ran AI on hardware they own. No slideware marathons — every session leaves you with something you can use.

⚙️

Technical track

Deep, generic-enough-to-reuse talks on Kubernetes, storage, networking, virtualization, edge and AI infra — not just Cozystack.

📈

Business track

Case studies with numbers: private cloud in banks, hosting-to-cloud, universities, marketplaces and the economics of open source.

🎙️

More than talks

Fireside interviews, live Q&A, online networking rooms, a community job board and giveaways across both days.
