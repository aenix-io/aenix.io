What we’re looking for

DEEP. USEFUL.
NOT ONLY ABOUT US.

Yes, we build Cozystack and the AENIX platform. But this is a community conference. We welcome strong, vendor-neutral talks for the whole ecosystem, alongside real stories from people running open clouds.

01 / Technical track

  • Kubernetes internals, operators & controllers
  • Storage: LINSTOR/DRBD, Ceph, SeaweedFS & CSI
  • Networking: Cilium, Kube-OVN & multi-tenant fabrics
  • Virtualization, KubeVirt & VM/container convergence
  • GPU tenancy, inference serving & AI infrastructure
  • Platform engineering, IDPs & GitOps
  • Edge & distributed clusters
  • Observability, databases on Kubernetes & security
  • Open-source contributions & the CNCF landscape

02 / Business & case studies

  • Migrating off VMware: the plan, numbers & pain
  • Launching a public cloud on open source
  • Hosting provider → cloud provider monetization
  • Private cloud in regulated industries
  • Research & university computing on open source
  • Sovereign cloud & data-residency strategy
  • Selling through a cloud marketplace as a partner
  • FinOps, billing & unit economics for a k8s cloud
  • How teams actually adopt AI internally

Not on the list? If your idea helps people build or run cloud-native platforms, submit it anyway. Depth and honesty beat topic-fit.

Session formats

Pick the shape
that fits your idea.

01 / ~30 min

Conference talk

About 30 minutes including live Q&A. The main format for both tracks.

02 / ~10 min

Lightning talk

One sharp idea in about 10 minutes. A great format for a first-time speaker.

03 / 30–40 min

Panel / fireside

A hosted conversation. Propose the topic and who you’d want to join.

Sessions are delivered live where possible. Some may be pre-recorded with the speaker live for Q&A. See the speaker handbook for details.
