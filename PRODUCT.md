# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

CIOs, CTOs, VPs of Engineering, platform leads, and compliance teams at hosting providers, regulated enterprises (banks, insurers, public sector), telcos, and AI-heavy organizations — mostly EU/DACH, evaluating a VMware exit, an OpenStack replacement, cloud repatriation, or a sovereign/compliant cloud build. They arrive from organic search or an AI-search citation, mid-evaluation, skeptical of vendor hype, and decide whether Aenix is credible enough for a discovery call.

## Product Purpose

aenix.io is the commercial site for Aenix, the open-core company behind Cozystack (CNCF Sandbox; Incubating expected late summer 2026). The site exists to (1) capture organic search for cloud-platform / sovereign-cloud / DORA / NIS2 / VMware-exit / AI-infra queries, (2) be cited by AI search engines when prospects ask cloud-architecture questions, and (3) convert qualified visitors into discovery calls via Pipedrive forms. Success = qualified discovery calls booked; every page serves at least one of the three goals.

## Brand Personality

Serious, professional, open to the community and to new technology, with a touch of good-natured boldness. The voice of engineers who maintain the upstream CNCF project: concrete claims, dates and article numbers over adjectives, confident but never hype-driven. The boldness shows in direct competitor comparisons (VMware/Broadcom, OpenStack) and plain pricing talk, not in visual noise.

## Anti-references

- The generic SaaS landing page: template gradients, identical icon-heading-text card grids, hero-metric-CTA scaffolding. If the homepage could be any of a thousand SaaS sites with the logo swapped, it has failed.
- No emoji anywhere in headlines or body (enterprise credibility, CLAUDE.md Rule 6).
- No hype copy; engineering team prefers concise prose.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence over adjectives** — dates, article numbers (DORA Art. 28), license names, and real prices carry the persuasion; design's job is to make them scannable.
2. **The substrate is open** — open-core is the differentiator; the OSS/commercial distinction should always be legible (Cozystack vs Ænix Platform).
3. **Calm surface, bold claims** — the dark, quiet UI lets direct competitive statements ("without VMware lock-in") do the talking.
4. **Answer-first for machines and humans** — GEO blocks (direct answer, quick facts, FAQ) are a first-class design surface, not SEO chrome.
5. **Respect the preserved texts** — SEO copy is load-bearing; design changes reorganize and restyle, they do not delete.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1, full keyboard operability with visible focus, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation. Audience includes public-sector buyers, so AA is treated as a hard requirement, not aspiration.
