Sources: 1. front matter robots: "noindex, ..." 2. layouts that hard-code a noindex meta (keep in sync with grep -rn "noindex" layouts --include='*.html' | grep -v partials/seo) 3. calculator-app host pages unless `indexable: true` 4. utility URLs with no content of their own (below), and everything under /workshop/ (hands-on lab material for workshop attendees) 5. the "types" taxonomy (post-type filter pages, not topics) 6.
