## Engagement model

1. **Discovery call** (30 min, free) — confirm fit, scope, regulatory context, platform match
2. **Platform Readiness Assessment** (14 or 28 days, fixed price agreed up front) — current-state audit, platform recommendation, roadmap
3. **Build** — Public Cloud Platform at provider scale: live in weeks once the hardware is ready, via the productized installer. Private Cloud Platform: a 3-12 month build depending on scope. Multi-region national or operator programmes: a 3-6 month pilot, then 9-18 months to full production
4. **Managed operations** (ongoing retainer) — Ænix runs the platform, or a hybrid model
