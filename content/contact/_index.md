**On the call we'll cover:**

- What you want to achieve — sell cloud, replace VMware, run GPUs, or get support for your own Cozystack
- Which Ænix platform fits — Public Cloud, Private Cloud or AI — or enterprise support for Cozystack
- Regulatory context (DORA, NIS2, sector rules, national sovereignty requirements)
- Current architecture and migration scope
- Next step: usually a Platform Readiness Assessment (14 or 28 days, fixed price)
- Which [support tier](/pricing/) matches your needs, and the timeline

No commitment. If Ænix isn't the right fit, we'll say so directly.

If you would like an in-depth architecture review instead, mention "architecture review" in the message field and we'll bring in a solutions engineer.
