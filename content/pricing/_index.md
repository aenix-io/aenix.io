## How pricing works

Two independent choices:

- **Platform** — which of the three Ænix platforms you run, decided by who consumes the capacity: customers who are not you (Public Cloud Platform), your own regulated business units (Private Cloud Platform), or AI workloads on your own GPUs (AI Platform).
- **Support tier** — how Ænix supports you. Four tiers, from Basic ($1,250 per 10 nodes per month) to Enterprise (custom).

Services — Platform Readiness Assessment, pilot, build, migration, managed operations — are scoped separately.

**Which side you land on:** an Ænix Public Cloud Platform subscription at provider scale and support for self-run Cozystack are productized, so the price list below is the price. Private Cloud Platform, AI Platform and multi-region operator builds are programmes, not SKUs: they always include an assessment, a migration plan and an operating model, so they are quoted per RFP after scoping.

## What your estate actually costs

The tiers are priced **per 10 physical nodes**, so the list price is arithmetic rather than a starting point to negotiate from. A worked example at annual-billing prices, per month:

| Physical nodes | Packages | Basic | Standard | Plus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1 | $1,250 | $3,000 | $5,500 |
| 20 | 2 | $2,500 | $6,000 | $11,000 |
| 40 | 4 | $5,000 | $12,000 | $22,000 |
| 60 | 6 | $7,500 | $18,000 | $33,000 |

Two honest caveats. Nodes round up to the next package — 44 nodes is five, not 4.4. And this covers the subscription only: migration, a Platform Readiness Assessment, a pilot or managed operations are separate and quoted after scoping, because they depend on what you are moving and how much of the running you want us to do. Work outside the tier's scope is billed at $150 per hour.

If your estate is large enough that the table above stops looking sensible, that is the point where the conversation becomes a programme rather than a price list, and we would rather scope it than have you multiply your way to a number neither of us would stand behind.

## Services (priced separately)

### Platform Readiness Assessment

A fixed-price engagement in two variants: 14 days (focused, one workstream) or 28 days (full, all workstreams and a Phase 2 roadmap). It produces a written current-state review, target architecture, migration roadmap and risk register. The price is agreed before it starts.

[Platform Readiness Assessment →](/services/platform-readiness-assessment/)

### Pilot

A defined slice — one workload class, one customer cohort, one site — used for multi-region operator programmes, which typically run a 3-6 month pilot. Quoted once the slice is defined.

### Platform build

- **Ænix Public Cloud Platform at provider scale:** productized installer, live in weeks once the hardware is ready.
- **Multi-region national or operator programmes:** a 3-6 month pilot, then 9-18 months to full multi-region production.
- **Ænix Private Cloud Platform:** after the assessment, a 3-12 month build depending on scope.

Programme builds are quoted per RFP; the drivers are estate size, migration volume, regulatory scope and how much of the operating model you want us to run.

### Managed operations

A monthly retainer after deployment: platform operations, monitoring, incident response, capacity planning and quarterly architecture reviews. Set with the build scope.
