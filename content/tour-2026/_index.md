<h2 class="ws-h2" id="tour-formats-h">What we can do together</h2>
<p class="ws-lead">Pick a format, or combine several. The length is up to you — from a 30-minute conversation to a half-day session. Talks for universities and communities, coffee and podcast recordings are free.</p>
<div class="tour-formats">
<article class="tour-format">
<span class="tour-format__who">Seminar · universities, startups, communities</span>
<h3>Open source as a business model</h3>
<p>How an open-source project turns into a company: licences, community, the open-core model and its trade-offs, and the mistakes we would not repeat — told from building Cozystack and Ænix.</p>
</article>
<article class="tour-format">
<span class="tour-format__who">Talk · engineering teams, labs</span>
<h3>AI and GPUs on your own infrastructure</h3>
<p>How Cozystack pools the GPUs you own into isolated tenant clusters and serves models behind an inference endpoint — an AI platform on hardware you control, without a second stack.</p>
</article>
<article class="tour-format">
<span class="tour-format__who">Talk · infrastructure teams, providers</span>
<h3>Building a modern private cloud</h3>
<p>Virtual machines, Kubernetes on demand, managed databases and storage on one open platform — and an honest look at moving off VMware: what moves easily and what does not.</p>
</article>
<article class="tour-format">
<span class="tour-format__who">Talk · maintainers, foundations, students</span>
<h3>Becoming a CNCF project — and why bother</h3>
<p>What the CNCF sandbox and incubation process really asks of a project, what it changes for users and contributors, and when it is worth it. First-hand, from going through it with Cozystack.</p>
</article>
<article class="tour-format">
<span class="tour-format__who">Hands-on · your company or class</span>
<h3>A small in-house workshop</h3>
<p>A 90-minute to half-day lab for your team or students: everyone gets a personal environment on a real cluster in the browser and builds a working cloud setup. Nothing to install.</p>
</article>
<article class="tour-format">
<span class="tour-format__who">Informal · anyone</span>
<h3>Just a coffee and a chat</h3>
<p>No agenda required. Infrastructure, open source, startups, the cloud market, hiring, or how things work where you are — I'm happy to listen and share what I've seen.</p>
</article>
