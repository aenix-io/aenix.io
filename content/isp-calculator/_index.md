If you sell cloud to your own customers, the question is not what a platform costs but what a node earns. This calculator models the monthly P&L: the infrastructure footprint you run, the services you sell on top of it (managed Kubernetes, VMs, databases, GPU, object storage), how full those nodes actually are, what the team costs, and what the same business looks like before and after moving to Cozystack. Multi-currency, and it exports a PDF you can put in front of a board.
