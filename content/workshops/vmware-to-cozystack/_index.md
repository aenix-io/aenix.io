## Why this tour, why now

Broadcom's acquisition rewired VMware's economics: perpetual licenses were discontinued, subscriptions start at 72 cores per order, and documented renewal increases range from 150% to 1500% — from AT&T's lawsuit to public complaints by CISPE members. vSphere 7 general support ended on October 2, 2025, so staying put now has a price tag too.

The technical alternative exists — VMs as first-class Kubernetes objects, on open source, on your own hardware. What's usually missing is the confidence that *your* team can run it. That is what a workshop fixes better than any whitepaper: in half a day, every participant migrates a VM off a live ESXi with their own hands and assembles a self-service platform around it — storage, networking, managed PostgreSQL, monitoring, backup, GitOps.

## The program in one paragraph

A reality check on VMware economics (the only slide block of the day), then a vSphere → Cozystack translation map you verify live in your own tenant, then the core: live export from a running ESXi, virt-v2v conversion, and your own migrated VM answering requests. After that — the platform part: managed PostgreSQL in three minutes, LoadBalancer, out-of-the-box Grafana monitoring, Velero backup, and the whole stack from one Git manifest. The day closes with an honest-limitations block (what does not migrate 1:1, and when we advise against migrating), a pilot playbook for your own datacenter, and 45 minutes of open networking. Full agenda on each city page.

## After the workshop — the part most events skip

- **30-day personal demo environment** on a real cluster, so the skills survive the week after.
- **Support chat with Cozystack maintainers for 30 days** — direct answers from the people who write the platform.
- **Take-home labs** extending the in-room build.
- **An official Cozystack certificate** after completing the labs — free.
